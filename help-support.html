About Matrix Marketplace

Matrix Marketplace is a student-focused platform designed to make buying and selling easier, help student sellers reach customers, and bring the campus community together. We are building it in stages, so some features may still be in development.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Open the sign-in page and follow the sign-up steps. Choose the account role that matches how you want to use the marketplace. You can use the buyer and seller options if you want to do both.

How do I become a seller?

Sign in with a seller-enabled account and open My Store. Complete your store details, then use the add-product option to create a listing.

How do I find or buy a product?

Browse the homepage or Categories, open a product to review its details, add it to your cart and follow the checkout steps.

Where can I check my orders?

Open the orders area from your account or checkout page to review the orders associated with your account.

How do I update my products?

Open My Store, go to My Products and select the listing you want to update. You can manage available product details and stock there.

What if I have a payment or delivery problem?

Contact Customer Service and include your order details. Do not assume an order is paid until the platform clearly confirms its payment status.

Report a Problem

Tell us what went wrong. Choose a topic and describe the issue; the form will prepare a WhatsApp message for Customer Service.

WhatsApp will open so you can review and send the message. Do not share passwords, PINs or one-time verification codes.

Safety Guidelines

  • Check product descriptions, prices and seller details before ordering.
  • Keep conversations and order information available in case you need support.
  • Never share your password, payment PIN or one-time verification code.
  • Be cautious of requests to pay outside the platform or through unfamiliar links.
  • When arranging collection, choose a public, appropriate meeting point and let someone you trust know.
  • Report suspicious listings or behaviour to Customer Service.

How Matrix Works

  • Buyers: Browse categories, view product details, add items to the cart and place an order.
  • Sellers: Set up a store, publish product listings and manage stock and orders from My Store.
  • Updates: Check Notifications for activity related to your account.

Payment processing and some marketplace features may still be in development. An order being created does not by itself mean payment has been received.

Privacy & Account Security

  • Use a strong, unique password and keep it private.
  • Only provide information needed to use the marketplace.
  • Avoid posting private contact, financial or identity details in public product listings or community posts.
  • Contact Customer Service if you notice activity on your account that you do not recognise.
